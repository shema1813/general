All About Apples

Sweet, Juicy, and Healthy!

Apples are one of the most popular fruits in the world. They come in many different colors, flavors, and sizes. You can eat them fresh, bake them into pies, or make them into juice.

Types of Apples

There are many types of apples. Here are some of the most famous ones:

  • Red Delicious – Sweet and bright red.
  • Granny Smith – Green and sour, great for baking.
  • Golden Delicious – Yellow and very soft.
  • Fuji – Crisp and very sweet.
  • Honeycrisp – Juicy with a perfect crunch.

Health Benefits

Apples are packed with vitamins and fiber. They help your body stay strong and healthy. Eating apples regularly can:

  1. Improve your digestion
  2. Boost your immune system
  3. Keep your heart healthy
  4. Give you energy

Fun Fact

Did you know that there are over 7,500 varieties of apples grown around the world?
