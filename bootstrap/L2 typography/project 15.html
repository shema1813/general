Types of Aircraft

Commercial Airliners
These large airplanes, such as the Boeing 787 and Airbus A350, are designed to carry hundreds of passengers while maintaining fuel efficiency and top-level safety.
Military Aircraft
Planes like the F-22 Raptor and Eurofighter Typhoon are engineered for speed, stealth, and advanced combat performance.
Helicopters
Helicopters are unique due to their ability to hover, making them essential for rescue missions, news broadcasting, and military operations.
