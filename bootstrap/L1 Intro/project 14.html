All About Cats

Cats are amazing animals known for their independence, agility, and affection. They communicate using meows, purrs, and body language.

Fun Cat Facts

  • Cats have over 100 vocal sounds.
  • They can jump up to 6 times their height.
  • Purring can help them heal.
  • Each cat's nose print is unique.
